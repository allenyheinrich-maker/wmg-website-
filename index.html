About Us

White Mother is a community-based organization registered in Tanzania. We provide practical vocational skills and second chances to vulnerable young women and school dropouts (aged 12+) so they can build dignified, independent futures.

Our Mission

To give 100 young women in Songea a real second chance at economic independence through practical skills and community-run enterprises.

Our Vision

A community where every young woman has the skills, confidence, and opportunity to support herself and contribute to society.

Governance & Roles

White Mother is governed by a board and managed by a leadership team. Clear roles help us remain accountable to our beneficiaries, donors, and partners.

NameRoleKey Responsibilities
Frank Mtweve Chairman Overall controller and overseer of all organizational operations. Provides final approval on budgets, programs, partnerships, and major decisions. Represents the organization at the highest level. Ensures the board fulfills its duties and that the organization stays true to its mission.
Henrick Komba Project Coordinator Connects with donors and partners, maintains donor relationships, and prepares funding proposals and reports. Provides advice and recommendations to the Chairman. Updates and maintains organizational documents, records, and project reports. Coordinates day-to-day project activities and ensures timely reporting.
[Name] [Board Member] [e.g., Oversees finances / legal compliance / community relations]
Good practice note: For strong governance, the Chairman (overall overseer) and the Project Coordinator (donor liaison and document manager) should work closely together while maintaining clear separation of duties. Financial approvals, donor receipts, and project reports should be reviewed by at least two people. This protects the organization, builds donor trust, and prevents errors or misuse.

Board meetings occur quarterly to review progress, finances, and safeguarding. Minutes are kept and shared with relevant partners on request.

Safeguarding & Child Protection

White Mother works with young women and school dropouts, including minors. We are committed to protecting every person who comes into contact with our organization.

  • We do not tolerate any form of abuse, exploitation, harassment, or discrimination.
  • All staff and volunteers must sign and follow our Safeguarding and Child Protection Policy.
  • We obtain informed consent before using any photos or stories of beneficiaries.
  • We have a named Safeguarding Focal Person: [Name, phone, email].
  • Anyone can report a concern confidentially. Reports will be taken seriously and handled promptly.
