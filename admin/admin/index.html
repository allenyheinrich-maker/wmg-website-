Who We Are

Established in 2015 in Songea, Ruvuma region, the White Mothers Group (WMG) is a community-rooted organization dedicated to restoring dignity and economic independence to vulnerable young women and school dropouts (aged 12+). We provide practical, life-changing skills to help our community thrive.

WMG currently runs established vocational training programs in tailoring, carpentry, welding, computer literacy, plumbing, and mechanics, alongside existing small-scale beekeeping activities.

Why We Do It

Early pregnancy is one of the biggest reasons girls leave school in Tanzania. According to the national audit office, 42,954 girls dropped out in a single year. Ruvuma has the second-highest rate of teenage childbearing in Tanzania at 37%. Without a pathway back to education and income, these young women face long-term exclusion and dependency. WMG provides that second chance.

Our Mission

To give 100 young women in Songea a real second chance at economic independence. We do this by providing practical vocational skills, establishing community-run enterprises, and empowering young mothers with the tools to support themselves and their families.

Our Future: The Beekeeping Enterprise

Demand has outgrown our centre's equipment. To secure a reliable income, WMG is establishing a community-run beekeeping enterprise of 150 Langstroth hives. Supported by NEST Projects Foundation and Tunza Nyuki, this project will generate an estimated 45 to 60 million TZS in annual honey sales, funding the training centre and providing income for the women.

Our Work in Action

Accomplishments & Future Plans

Our Accomplishments

Since 2015, we have successfully supported vulnerable young women through our vocational training centre. Our new community-run beekeeping enterprise, supported by NEST Projects Foundation, will establish 150 Langstroth hives, training 100 young women and generating an estimated 45 to 60 million TZS in annual honey sales.

Plans for Success

To ensure the success of this venture, WMG plans to:

1. Train 100 participants in technical beekeeping and financial literacy.
2. Develop products like raw honey, beeswax candles, and natural balms.
3. Establish internal Savings & Loan Groups to foster long-term financial independence.

How Training is Delivered

Will WMG deliver the trade and business training?

Answer: Yes, WMG will take the lead in delivering the core training. Because we have been established since 2015, we have our own in-house trainers and workshop facilities in Songea.

For the technical beekeeping and product development training, WMG will work in tandem with Tunza Nyuki, our specialized technical partner who provides expert facilitators, mentors, and market connections. Overall project funding and oversight are provided by NEST Projects Foundation.
